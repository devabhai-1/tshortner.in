Frequently Asked Questions

  • Why does T-Player ask for media permissions?
    T-Player runs locally on your phone. It needs media permissions only to show and play your local videos, photos, and music files without uploading anything.
  • Does T-Player upload or store my private files?
    No. Your media remains 100% on your device storage. We have no servers storing user files.
  • How can I report a playback bug or unsupported format?
    Please email support@tshortner.in with your Android device model and the file format extension (.mkv, .avi, etc.).
