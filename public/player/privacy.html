Privacy Policy for T-Player

Core Privacy Commitment: T-Player operates completely on-device. We do NOT collect, harvest, store, or sell your personal details, media files, photos, or documents to remote servers or third parties.

1. What T-Player is

T-Player is an advanced multimedia player and file viewer designed for Android devices. It plays videos and audio files, previews photos with pinch-to-zoom, inspects text/code/log files, and streams media from user-entered URLs. T-Player functions as a standalone utility tool and requires no user registration, login, or account.

2. Permissions and Data Access

T-Player requests only permissions necessary to deliver local media and file viewing features:

  • Videos & Photos (READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_MEDIA_VISUAL_USER_SELECTED): Required to scan and display local media files stored in your phone folders (Camera, Download, Movies, Pictures). Media files remain strictly on your device and are never uploaded.
  • Audio Files (READ_MEDIA_AUDIO): Required to index and play music and audio tracks stored on device storage.
  • Storage / File Access (READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): Used when picking text files, logs, or documents (.txt, .json, .log, .pdf) for local preview.
  • Internet & Network (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Used solely when you enter an external streaming URL to stream remote media.
  • Foreground Service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK, POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Allows audio or media playback to continue uninterrupted in the background while your phone screen is turned off.
  • Screen Wake Lock (WAKE_LOCK): Keeps your screen awake while watching a video.

3. What We Never Do

  • No Personal Data Collection: No names, email addresses, phone numbers, or device tracking IDs are collected.
  • No Data Monetization: We never sell, rent, or trade your media or data to third-party data brokers.
  • No Analytics or Tracking SDKs: We do not track your viewing habits or monitor your local files.
  • No Background Surveillance: T-Player never accesses your camera, microphone, contacts, or location.

4. Third-Party URLs & Streaming

When you enter a web streaming link, your device connects directly to that remote server to stream media. T-Player has no control over third-party servers, and their data practices are governed by their respective privacy policies.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR)

T-Player is safe for all audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13, nor does the app contain content directed specifically at children.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Because all media processing is performed locally on your device and no data is transmitted to cloud servers, there is no remote data to retain or delete. You can clear local app preferences at any time through Android System Settings.

7. Changes to Policy

If our privacy practices change, this policy will be updated with a new revision date. Continued use of T-Player after updates indicates acceptance.
